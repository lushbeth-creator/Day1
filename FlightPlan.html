Foundation before solutioning.

The sequence is deliberate. Pilots first learn how a transaction works, then why the ecosystem carries risk, then how controls such as underwriting, KYC, PCI DSS and monitoring manage that risk. Only then do we apply those ideas to the choices a Pilot makes in the field.

Follow the payment

Trace authorization from customer to issuer and understand the role of the merchant, gateway, processor, acquirer and networks.

Find the exposure

Separate authorization from settlement and finality. Learn why an approved transaction can still create loss later.

Manage the risk

Connect merchant verification, accurate applications, fraud controls, PCI DSS and monitoring to the same risk lifecycle.
